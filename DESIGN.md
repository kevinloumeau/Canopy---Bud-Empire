## Night lighting

Night is one deferred light map, not a stack of overlays. The backdrop stays the same quiet studio gradient, deepened and cooled to a dark teal-slate, and the halo behind the shop becomes the building's own spill; there is no literal sky, no stars and no moon. The diorama is painted on its own transparent surface, and a single light map is multiplied over it, masked to the diorama's alpha so the backdrop is never tinted: an ambient of deep cool ink that darkens toward the frame edges, a broad room fill per floor (warm on the shop floor and mezzanine, purple in the grow room) so the building glows like a lantern while the street goes dark, and every fixture added into the map as coloured light, so lit floor keeps its real wood colour and overlapping fixtures sum instead of stacking discs. All fixtures live in one list (`nightFixtures`): street sconces, bollards, the exit lamp and the storefront's spill onto the sidewalk; slab downlights, counter and floor pools, the vitrine and the lounge door; a work light over every station (cool over the grow room); wall washes on every rear wall; the kiosk sign; the purple grow pendants with their cones; and the stair treads. Above the map: one soft cone and halo per fixture as light in the air plus a purple haze over the grow room; an emissive buffer added over the map. Emitters declare themselves as the day pass draws them (`lit()`, with `litEllipse`, `litLine` and `litBox` for faces; `warmStrip()` declares itself), and while the night scene is painted each one is painted again into a buffer that runs alongside the scene. Every opaque polygon, ellipse, thick line and the blocks and limbs of every person drawn afterwards paint black over that buffer, so an emitter behind a wall, a counter or a guest is hidden in the buffer exactly as it is in the scene; translucent surfaces (glass hoods, shadows, washes) leave it alone. Nothing keeps a second list of positions: globe pendants, bench and street lamp faces, bollard caps, downlight trims, station screens and status lamps, case and vitrine LED bars, shelf and merchandise-bay strips, the grow racks' bars, the pendants' LED rows, the neon leaf, the lounge lamps, the self-order kiosks' backlit screens and status lights, and every fascia, counter, arch and stair strip. Delivery drones are drawn inside the night scene so they darken like everything else, with their red, green and white navigation lights declared as emitters. Night is carried by points of light, not lines: lamp faces go into the buffer at full strength; the concealed strips under every shelf, fascia, counter front and stair tread go in as faint trim at under 20% so the floors do not become a grid of glowing lines (only the ORDER and PICKUP arches' strips count as signage and stay full); spill (glows and cones) stays out of the buffer, since the light map already lifts what it lands on. The buffer is added with `lighter`, then the signs, marquee, kiosk readout and LOUNGE neon, which are drawn as text, are redrawn explicitly. A bloom of the buffer and those texts is drawn at half resolution, blurred with the canvas filter and added back (skipped where the filter is unsupported); and dust drifting down through the pendants' and street lamps' beams. Dusk and dawn scale the ambient, the fixtures, the backdrop and the bloom by the same amount. Lamp breathing and dust hold still under reduced motion. Branch maps use the same backdrop treatment and light map: their theme colour deepens the same way, and every `glow()` they draw registers as a fixture for the map, the halos, the lamp faces and the bloom.

## Organic graphics language

The renderer's geometry is softened everywhere rather than per prop, all seeded deterministically (`seedNoise` of a world identity) so nothing shimmers between frames or while panning:

- **Light-aware edges, not wireframes.** `drawBox` no longer outlines every face; only the top edges facing the key light catch a `#ffffff30` highlight, and the dark sides are defined by the gradient shading and contact shadows.
- **Bevelled furniture.** Furniture-scale boxes draw their top as a slightly lighter rim with the surface inset ~1–2px, so counters and machines read rounded. The same size gate drives a quantized per-floor-cell tint nudge (±3%), so one machine's parts match while its twin across the room drifts a shade; architecture keeps exact colours.
- **Blob silhouettes.** `blobEllipse` (eight seeded lobes joined by quadratics, same radial shading and night occlusion as `ellipse`) replaces perfect ovals for foliage, soil, boulders and canopies; `leafShape` draws pointed leaves and calyxes. Trees carry escaping rim leaves; buds are stacked calyx teardrops with curling pistils (canvas `flowerBud` and the `strainBud` SVG thumbnail alike). Branch scenes reach these through `api.blob` / `api.leaf` / `api.noise`.
- **Material everywhere.** The mottled grain tile now also covers mid-size furniture faces and the upper floor slabs at reduced alpha; queue ropes sag on a deeper catenary.
- **Grounding and atmosphere.** Branch trees, pines and bushes cast soft down-left contact shadows; branch lawns get a seeded meadow scatter (grass tufts, occasional wildflower dots) and the desert gets pebbles and scour patches, drawn right after the ground slab so structures painted later cover them. The main map's backdrop carries two vast world-anchored tone shifts (a lift toward the key light, a cooler settle low-left), and `atmosphereFinish` ends every frame — main map and branches — with a faint warm screen-blended wash from the key light's corner at day plus a whisper of world-anchored grain.

All of it costs roughly what the old wireframe strokes did (~3.5ms software-rendered frames at 1440×900).

A close-zoom pass adds: queue ropes and stanchions cast soft down-left floor shadows; the furniture bevel rim is lit directionally (dim on the shadow side, bright toward the key light) so pale counter tops read as rounded lips, not rings; large pale slab tops carry two faint seeded stone veins once `unit ≥ 22`; the globe pendants are shaded spheres (amber rim, cream body, upper-left bloom, small specular) instead of flat discs; and the Seeds bench trays sprout twin pointed cotyledons.

Branch buildings' warm window panes (tones from `#e0`) register as dim emitters through `api.lit(paint,'pane')` at emissive weight .26, so occupied windows keep a soft interior glow — with bloom, without whiting out — after dark, while dark panes go properly black under the night multiply.
