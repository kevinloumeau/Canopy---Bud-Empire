Privacy and support

Canopy: Bud Empire
Updated 9 November 2026

Canopy is a single-player idle game. There is no account, no sign‑in, and we never ask for your name, email or location.

Your game

Your shop is saved on your own device. It is never uploaded to us. Reset game in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app keeps a short list of purchase receipts on your device so that Restore purchases can give you back what you bought.

Reminders

If you turn on Reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device, such as when a build is done or your back-room safe is full. They are not sent through a server, and you can turn them off at any time.

Anonymous gameplay counts

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy, Canopy may send anonymous counts, such as “a session started” or “a goal was finished”, to TelemetryDeck. Each install gets a random number that is scrambled before it leaves the device. These counts are never linked to you, never used for advertising, and never sold.

What we never do

  • Track you across other apps or websites.
  • Use the advertising identifier.
  • Sell or share your data with data brokers.

Age

Canopy is a work of fiction about running a dispensary, made for players 21 and over. Nothing in the game is for sale, and no real product is involved. Your answer to the age question stays on your device.

Contact and support

Questions, bugs or a lost purchase? Write to us on the Canopy support page and we will get back to you.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
