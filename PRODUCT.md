# Dispensary Idle

A mobile-first interactive isometric dispensary game. Six upgradeable stations turn seeds into customer sales. Preserve queues, employee upgrades, online orders, saves, pause and 1×/2×/4× speed, pan and zoom. Canvas 2D and the classic Safari-compatible build follow the prior iPhone failures. Publication remains pending explicit approval of the remote push.

## Confirmed visual direction

The latest request opens the dispensary into a multi-floor architectural cutaway inspired by the supplied office interior. Preserve the green dispensary identity and gameplay while exposing rooms, shelves, workstations, glass partitions, and connecting stairs. Use warm lighting, timber, pale stone, forest-green walls, and a muted dark backdrop.
