# Shop activity extension

Mode: Experience with lightweight operating actions. Scope: Budtender Moments, an active-play shift recap, and existing-operations alerts; no new tray tab, currency, reward destination or redesign.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the existing automated shop feel responsive to people and operations through occasional optional recommendations and a short account of the player's shift.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the forest-green controls, pale mint text and brass attention color, Bricolage typography and existing 44px touch targets. The map remains the dominant surface.

STORY: Notice a customer's speech bubble, recommend an unlocked product, see a reaction and tip at pickup. Review five active minutes, then follow one useful next action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single compact Shop activity control sits below the HUD, opposite the map controls. The customer bubble tracks the existing world projection. Opening activity reveals one narrow scrollable panel with the request first, recap second, and actionable store alerts last.

FORM: Local extension of the established game surface; no concept seed or new identity. Ignore or close a moment without forfeiting the customer's normal sale. Alerts open existing Stock or People controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
