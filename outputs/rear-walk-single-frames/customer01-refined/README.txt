Customer 01 - refined rear walk frames

Eight transparent 1024 x 1536 PNG frames, individually generated and registered.
Frame 03 has a lower reach and opposite arm swing. Frames 01 and 07 have shorter transition strides. Head size was adjusted in 01, 05, 06 and 07 using frame 00 as the fixed appearance reference.

All frames use uniform scale and translation, with collar y=352, waist y=770 and head center x=512. Maximum collar registration error is below 0.5 px. The moving feet were not used as bounding-box anchors.

The 8x1 atlas has eight 256 x 384 cells in order 00-07. There is no padding between cells.

Small generated differences in hair, fabric, limb shapes and edge color remain. Static sequence and transparency checked; final in-game timing, foot sliding and motion smoothness are not certified. No gameplay files were changed.

Image generation: built-in image_gen. Prompts and source provenance are in generation-prompts.json and manifest.json. Original draft preserved in the sibling customer01-draft folder.
