# Street sprites (brief section 6)

27 transparent PNGs. Generated with Ludo `image generate-with-style` plus the brief's "slightly muted" line, 4 candidates each. Style references: the hero tree for trees, lamp and planter; the hero car for vehicles and the bike; the hero counter for everything else.

- `street.tree.01` and `street.car.red.fr` are the approved hero sprites.
- `street.car.white.fr` is a horizontal mirror of `street.car.white.fl`, because Ludo kept painting the front-right white car red. Its light comes from the upper left instead of the upper right.
- `fr` means the car faces front-right, with headlights on the right; `fl` means front-left.
- The manhole cover is green rather than iron, and the bus shelter has only a little glass.
