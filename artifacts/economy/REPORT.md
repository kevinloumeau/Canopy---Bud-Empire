# Four-hour economy check

Actual simulate(.05), 1x; starts at all stations Lv10, staff3, one kiosk, security5. No online sales or branch purchases. Invest scenario buys cheapest bottleneck equipment, employee or global boost every minute when affordable.

| Product | No reinvestment: earnings | Reinvesting: earnings | Reinvesting: cash left |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Flower | $97,329 | $99,527 | $3,248 |
| Pre-rolls | $144,495 | $147,411 | $608 |
| Edibles | $209,270 | $215,296 | $19,344 |

This fixture exposes diminishing returns: buying the cheapest bottleneck equipment, employee or boost increased four-hour revenue only about 2–3%. Travel and counter access remain real limits. Product choice changes margins substantially; packing-heavy products can also change the early bottleneck. This is a bounded midgame comparison, not proof that the full economy is balanced. It excludes branch investment, online dispatch, strain upgrades, milestone claims, timed-event participation and prestige.

Global boosts now cost 250 × 2.25^level and stop at level 12. Existing higher levels are retained. Additional contract rewards and optional prestige extend progression; they do not eliminate the physical service ceiling.
