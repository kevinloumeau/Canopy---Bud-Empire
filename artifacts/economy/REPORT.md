# Four-hour economy check

Actual simulate(.05), 1x; starts at all stations Lv10, staff3, one kiosk, security5. No online sales or branch purchases. The invest scenarios spend every minute when affordable.

| Product | No reinvestment | Old strategy: cheapest bottleneck buy | Following the recommendation | Gain over no reinvestment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flower | $1,024,121 | $1,134,281 | $2,269,840 | +122% |
| Pre-rolls | $1,224,654 | $1,291,136 | $2,451,230 | +100% |
| Edibles | $1,426,864 | $1,573,190 | $3,240,170 | +127% |

Earnings are lifetime revenue after four hours (October 16, 2026 run, Chromium).

The old bottleneck cue kept pointing at Pickup after it reached its handoff floor, where more levels cannot speed it up, so reinvesting added only 3–11%. The recommendation now tries every station level and shop upgrade, keeps the one that adds the most income per dollar (preferring what five minutes of income can buy), and steers money to bags per customer, arrivals and prices once a counter is capped. The Fix button on the bottleneck row and the Shop recommendation both follow it.

This is a bounded midgame comparison, not proof that the full economy is balanced. It excludes branch investment, online dispatch, strain upgrades, milestone claims, timed-event participation and prestige.
